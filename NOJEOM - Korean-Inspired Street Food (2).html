About NOJEOM

NOJEOM is inspired by the vibrant Korean street-food concept (Pojangmacha - 포장마차) and its energetic, welcoming street culture.

Our goal is to create a modern street-food brand that brings together familiar, comfort Indian snacks and popular fast food under one unique, stylish identity. We combine clean presentation with vibrant energy.
